# Acme Corporation — Expense & Reimbursement Policy

_Version 2.0, effective 1 February 2026. Owner: Finance._

## 1. Purpose

This policy explains which business expenses Acme reimburses, who approves them, and how to claim. It applies to all employees.

## 2. Approval limits

| Claim amount | Approved by |
| --- | --- |
| Up to 5,000 INR | Direct manager |
| 5,001 – 50,000 INR | Direct manager **and** Finance |
| Above 50,000 INR | Direct manager, Finance **and** the CFO |

Expenses above 50,000 INR should be pre-approved by the CFO **before** they are incurred.

## 3. Submitting a claim

- Claims must be submitted in the expense tool within **30 days** of the expense. Claims older than 30 days are rejected unless Finance grants an exception.
- Every claim needs an itemised receipt. A card statement alone is not a receipt.
- Approved claims are paid with the next monthly payroll.

## 4. Travel

- Domestic flights: economy class only.
- International flights longer than 8 hours: premium economy is allowed.
- Hotels: up to 7,000 INR per night in metro cities and 4,500 INR per night elsewhere.
- Daily meal allowance while travelling: 1,500 INR per day domestic, 60 USD per day international. Receipts are not required for the meal allowance.
- Local travel by cab is reimbursed; personal car use is reimbursed at 12 INR per km.

## 5. Not reimbursable

- Alcohol, except at client entertainment pre-approved by the CFO.
- Traffic or parking fines.
- Personal entertainment, gym memberships, or upgrades paid for personally.
- Lost or stolen personal items.

## 6. Work equipment

Employees may claim up to 3,000 INR per year for work accessories (headset, keyboard, mouse) with manager approval. Laptops and phones are provided by IT and are not claimable.
