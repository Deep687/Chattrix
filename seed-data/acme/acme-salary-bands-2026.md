# Acme Corporation — Salary Bands 2026

_Confidential. Upload with audience **Owners only**. Effective 1 April 2026. Owner: People Operations._

## 1. Purpose

These bands set the minimum and maximum annual base salary for each level. Offers and raises outside a band need written CFO sign-off.

## 2. Engineering bands (annual base, INR)

| Level | Title | Minimum | Maximum |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| E1 | Software Engineer I | 9,00,000 | 14,00,000 |
| E2 | Software Engineer II | 14,00,000 | 22,00,000 |
| E3 | Senior Software Engineer | 22,00,000 | 34,00,000 |
| E4 | Staff Engineer | 34,00,000 | 52,00,000 |

## 3. Annual review

Compensation is reviewed once a year in March. The merit budget for 2026 is 8% of total base payroll.
