# Globex Industries — Compensation Bands 2026

_Restricted. Upload with audience **Owners only**. Effective 1 January 2026. Owner: HR Compensation._

## 1. Scope

Bands apply to all full-time employees. A manager may offer up to the band midpoint; anything above the midpoint needs HR Director approval.

## 2. Bands (annual base, USD)

| Grade | Example role | Minimum | Midpoint | Maximum |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| G4 | Analyst | 58,000 | 68,000 | 78,000 |
| G5 | Senior Analyst | 74,000 | 87,000 | 100,000 |
| G6 | Manager | 96,000 | 113,000 | 130,000 |
| G7 | Senior Manager | 125,000 | 147,500 | 170,000 |

## 3. Equity

Grades G6 and above are eligible for an annual equity refresh, granted each February.
