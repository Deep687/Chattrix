# Globex Industries — Vendor Compliance Requirements

_Document GX-PRC-014, effective 1 April 2026. Owner: Procurement & Compliance._

## 1. Who this applies to

Any third party that supplies goods or services to Globex, or that processes Globex data.

## 2. Onboarding requirements

Before a purchase order is issued, every new vendor must provide:

1. A signed Globex Supplier Code of Conduct.
2. Proof of company registration and tax registration.
3. A completed security questionnaire, **if** the vendor will access Globex systems or data.
4. A current ISO 27001 certificate or SOC 2 Type II report, **if** the vendor will process Confidential or Restricted data.

Vendors that process personal data must also sign a Data Processing Agreement (DPA).

## 3. Risk tiers

| Tier | Criteria | Review frequency |
| --- | --- | --- |
| High | Processes Restricted data, or annual spend above 250,000 USD | Every 12 months |
| Medium | Accesses Globex systems, or annual spend 50,000–250,000 USD | Every 24 months |
| Low | Everything else | At contract renewal |

## 4. Approvals

- Contracts up to 50,000 USD per year: approved by the department head.
- Contracts above 50,000 USD per year: also approved by Procurement.
- High-tier vendors: also approved by the CISO.

## 5. Payments

Standard payment terms are **net 45 days** from a valid invoice. Changes to a vendor's bank details must be verified by a phone call to a known contact before any payment is made.

## 6. Offboarding

When a contract ends, the vendor must return or certify deletion of all Globex data within **30 days**, and all vendor access is revoked on the last day of the contract.
